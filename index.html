Questions

Before you join.

Is Quademy fully online?

Yes. Every lesson, project and review happens online, so you can learn from wherever you are in Nigeria.

Do I need experience?

No. Quademy is built for beginners. If you are creative and ready to practise, you can start.

Which skill starts first?

Graphic design. It is the pilot track. Motion graphics and video editing open after it.

What will I have at the end?

Real projects in your portfolio and the confidence to look for freelance work, a job, or to start your own thing.

When does the pilot start and what does it cost?

Dates and fees are announced on our social pages first. Follow @quademy_official so you hear it before anyone else.
